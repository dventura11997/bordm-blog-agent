# Bordmmm Writing Style Guide

Bordmmm is an Australian anime news and opinion site for dedicated anime fans. Every article should feel like it was written by someone who genuinely loves anime and has opinions worth hearing.

---

## GLOBAL STYLE RULES

**Voice:**
- Australian English throughout: favourite, colour, behaviour, recognise, honour
- Conversational but informed. Not academic. Not a press release.
- Never neutral on editorial pieces — take a position, defend it, be direct
- First-person plural (we/us) for opinion formats. Third-person for explainer/analysis.
- No em dashes. Use commas, colons, or full stops instead.

**Formatting conventions:**
- Italicise all character names and show names: *Tanjiro*, *Demon Slayer*, *Future Trunks*
- Bold key facts, stats, and conclusions: **She achieved $5.4 million on opening day**
- Use `---` horizontal rules between major sections for visual breathing room
- `## H2` for major sections, `### H3` for sub-sections
- Emojis only appear inside blockquotes — never in body text

**Blockquote conventions (use these exactly):**
- `> 🤯 [key reveal or most important insight]` — the big moment
- `> 📝 [spoiler warning or clarifying note]` — notes and caveats
- `> 🚨 Warning: Spoilers Ahead...` — spoiler header before major reveals
- `> 📣 "[character quote]" — Character Name` — direct in-series quotes
- `> 🙌 [celebratory or fan-favourite moment]` — hype moments

**Article openings:**
- News and longer pieces: open with an italic bullet summary list (4-6 items), then the lead paragraph
- Opinion pieces: open directly with the hook or the bold claim
- Never start with "In the world of anime..." or "When it comes to..."

---

## FORMAT 1: NEWS

Factual breaking story. Structured with H2 sections. Ends with a source hyperlink.

**Structure:**
1. Italic bullet summary (4-6 bullets in a `>` blockquote block or `*italic*` list)
2. Lead paragraph — key news in the first sentence
3. H2 sections for each angle (Release Date / Studio & Cast / Plot / Trailer)
4. `---` between sections
5. Blockquote for the single most important highlight
6. Closing line — excited but grounded. Link the source.

**Example — Blue Exorcist Season 3:**

*Summary:*
- *Blue Exorcist Season 3, the Shimane Illuminati Saga, is set to release in January 2024 after a six-year hiatus.*
- *The teaser trailer and key visual were unveiled at the AniPlex Summer Fest.*
- *The new season will maintain the original voice cast and is animated by Studio VOLN, a departure from A-1 Pictures.*
- *Based on manga volumes 10-15, the Shimane Illuminati arc focuses on Rin and Yukio's relationship.*

Prepare yourselves for the triumphant return of *Blue Exorcist* **after a six-year hiatus!** This action-packed fantasy series, adapted from *Kazue Katō's* manga, is making a comeback, promising a thrilling continuation of the *True Cross Academy saga.*

Fans have weathered the lengthy breaks before, with the first season airing in 2011 and its sequel, the *Kyoto Saga*, arriving six years later in 2017.

---

## Blue Exorcist Season 3 Release Date

The much-anticipated *Blue Exorcist Season 3*, aka the *Shimane Illuminati Saga*, **is set to grace our screens in January 2024.** While the exact date is yet to be confirmed, fans can mark their calendars for an early 2024 release. The announcement, accompanied by a teaser trailer and a captivating key visual, unfolded at the *AniPlex Summer Fest* event.

---

## Blue Exorcist Season 3 Studio and Voice Cast

*Blue Exorcist Season 3* will maintain continuity with its voice cast. The *Shimane Illuminati Saga* is undergoing animation at *Studio VOLN*, under the direction of *Daisuke Yoshida*. Notably, the shift from *A-1 Pictures*, which handled the previous seasons, raises questions about the change in studios.

---

## Blue Exorcist Season 3 Plot

*Blue Exorcist Season 3* will draw inspiration from volumes 10-15 (chapters 50-64) of the original manga, encompassing the *Shimane Illuminati arc*. The arc is celebrated for its intricate plot twists, while heavily spotlighting **Rin and Yukio's relationship.**

Get ready for the return of Blue Exorcist, where the Shimane Illuminati Saga is set to unfold, unravelling mysteries and captivating audiences once again!

## FORMAT 2: RANKING

Debates whether a character is the strongest, best, or most significant. Takes a clear position and defends it. No hedging.

**Structure:**
1. Italic bullet summary (4-5 bullets)
2. Optional spoiler warning blockquote if manga spoilers are involved
3. Opening that frames the debate and states the position
4. H2 sections each presenting a piece of evidence from the show
5. Blockquote for the key revelation moment
6. Closing that doubles down — not "we can see why fans disagree" but "this is settled"

**What NOT to do:** Never say "while some might argue..." or "it's subjective but...". The Bordmmm voice states its case and stands behind it.

**Example — Yuta Okkotsu Is The Strongest Sorcerer:**

*Summary:*
- *Yuta Okkotsu returns in Jujutsu Kaisen, showcasing his power in chapter #243.*
- *Yuta's quick and strategic strike against Kenjaku underscores his formidable sorcery skills.*
- *With Gojo's death, Yuta emerges as a crucial figure and potential replacement in the sorcerer hierarchy.*
- *The confrontation solidifies him as the series' new powerhouse.*

---

> 🚨 Warning: Spoilers Ahead...

In chapter #243 of *Jujutsu Kaisen*, the elusive mastermind *Kenjaku* makes a critical error. His intense focus on the battle against *Takaba* leads to a fatal lapse in his defences, allowing *Yuta* to swiftly incapacitate him with a single strike.

While it's true that this strategic move was made possible by *Takaba's* cursed technique, the significance lies in *Yuta* delivering the decisive blow. **Yuta emerges as the most promising candidate to fill the void left by his mentor.**

> 🤯 *Yuta's* resurgence underscores his newfound status as the most powerful sorcerer in Jujutsu Kaisen.

His defeat of *Kenjaku* in Chapter #243 showcases *Yuta's* exceptional abilities. Facing the formidable *"cursed technique reversal: anti-gravity,"* *Yuta* demonstrated both speed and strength, evading its full effects and **decapitating Kenjaku with a single swing.** Whether *Gege Akutami* will fully return to *Yuta's* initial story remains to be seen, but **his swift victory over *Kenjaku* unequivocally establishes Yuta as the new powerhouse in the world of Jujutsu Kaisen.**

## FORMAT 3: SPOTLIGHT

Celebrates a specific character moment, introduction, or defining trait. Personal, enthusiastic, scene-driven. This format lives and dies on how vividly it describes the moment.

**Structure:**
1. Italic bullet summary
2. Scene-setting intro — reference a video, a moment, or tell the reader to cast their mind back
3. `---` divider
4. H2 sections for each beat of the argument: The Unveiling, The Confrontation, The Impact
5. Bold for the single most iconic action in the scene
6. Conclusion that cements why this is the best ever — no wiggle room

**Example — Future Trunks Has The Best Character Introduction Of All Time:**

*Summary:*
- *Future Trunks' entrance was a shocking highlight in Dragon Ball Z.*
- *He fearlessly confronted Frieza and King Cold, showcasing a rare Super Saiyan transformation.*
- *His effortless victories over two of the series' most feared villains set a new benchmark.*
- *His debut set the standard for character entrances in anime.*

Before we get into more details, let's jog your memory of what happened all those years ago when *Future Trunks* was initially introduced into the *Dragon Ball Z* universe.

---

## The Unveiling of Future Trunks

*Future Trunks* burst onto the scene as a complete enigma, catching both the audience and the characters off guard. There's an undeniable acknowledgment that he is, without a doubt, among the elite character introductions in anime history.

---

## The Confrontation with Frieza and King Cold

Trunks' entrance into the Dragon Ball Z narrative was nothing short of spectacular. Fresh from the intense *Namek Saga*, where *Frieza* had previously wreaked havoc, the anticipation was palpable as the notorious tyrant returned, now accompanied by his formidable father, *King Cold*.

Facing *Frieza* with an audacious fearlessness, *Trunks* effortlessly dispatched *Frieza's* minions with a calm demeanour, setting the tone for the impending showdown. The unexpected turn of events reached its pinnacle when *Trunks*, with a confident smile, revealed his *Super Saiyan* transformation.

> 🙌 A feat that, during that period, still carried an air of rarity and awe.

---

## The Execution of Frieza and King Cold

What followed was a ruthless and one-sided battle. **Trunks sliced *Frieza* into pieces before obliterating him with a powerful blast, leaving no room for doubt about the young warrior's strength.**

But *Trunks* wasn't done yet. **The iconic moment where Trunks tossed his sword to King Cold, only to catch the swing and blast a hole through the tyrant's chest, was a testament to the character's audacity and unmatched combat prowess.**

The introduction of *Future Trunks* in *Dragon Ball Z* stands as one of the most memorable moments in the series. His fearless demeanour, jaw-dropping transformation, and decisive victories elevated him to legendary status. *Future Trunks'* arrival set the bar high for future character introductions in the world of anime and beyond.

## FORMAT 4: TOPLIST

Ranks a specific number of items. That number appears in the title and must exactly match the body. Casual, fun, and slightly irreverent in tone.

**Structure:**
1. Casual intro paragraph — set the vibe, can ask a rhetorical question
2. Optional honourable mentions (bullet list) before the countdown
3. `---` divider
4. H2 for each entry with the number and name: `## 5. Character Name:`
5. 2-3 sentences per entry — specific and punchy, not generic
6. Order from least to most significant — build to #1
7. No formal conclusion needed

**Tone note:** The intro is where Bordmmm's voice gets most casual. Real example: "Has anyone ever wondered why some of our favourite anime's ALWAYS have an incredibly perverted character?" That casualness is intentional.

## FORMAT 5: EXPLAINER

Breaks down how something happened or how a mechanic works in a show. Answers a specific "how" or "why" question. Informative but never dry. References specific chapters or episodes where known.

**Structure:**
1. Hook — frame the mystery or question clearly in the opening paragraph
2. Optional spoiler warning
3. H2 sections that walk through the explanation step by step
4. Blockquote for the major reveal
5. Bold for key facts and confirmed details
6. Closing that connects the explanation back to what it means for the character or story

**Tone:** More measured than spotlight. Think authoritative guide, not hype piece. Written in third-person.

**Example — How Muzan Accidentally Embraced Darkness:**

> 📝 Spoilers Ahead for Demon Slayer's Manga

*Muzan Kibutsuji*, the formidable antagonist in *Demon Slayer*, not only orchestrates the challenges that confront our heroes but also serves as the genesis of nearly every demon encountered. His unique ability to spawn demons raises an intriguing question: **if Muzan created the demons in the series, who, in turn, created Muzan?**

While *Demon Slayer* unfolds in the early 20th century, *Muzan's* existence **spans back a millennium**, revealed through flashbacks. Those unable to withstand his transformations find themselves reduced to dust. *Muzan* wields a surprising level of control over those he transforms, effortlessly dispatching them for mere infractions like uttering his name.

---

## The Unintentional Transformation

In **Chapter 127** of the *Demon Slayer* manga, it is unveiled that *Muzan's* journey into demonhood wasn't preordained.

> 🤯 Muzan's metamorphosis into a demon was a serendipitous accident.

Around the early 900s, *Muzan*, a human suffering from an unspecified ailment, opted for an experimental treatment to stave off death. The concoction, derived from the rare **Blue Spider Lily**, seemingly healed him. **However, it barred him from exposure to sunlight and triggered other demonic manifestations.** Enraged, *Muzan* slew the doctor responsible but remained incapable of tolerating sunlight.

---

## Muzan's Pivotal Quest for the Blue Spider Lily

Since that transformative incident, *Muzan* has pursued two primary objectives. First, **he seeks to proliferate demons by sharing his blood**, hoping that one might develop a tolerance to sunlight. Second, he relentlessly pursues the **Blue Spider Lily**, aiming to recreate the original serum.

**The poignant irony lies in the fact that the elusive Blue Spider Lily blooms during the day** — albeit rarely. Thus, regardless of how many millennia *Muzan* spent searching, he was fated never to discover it.

## FORMAT 6: ANALYSIS

Explores what makes a character, mechanic, or relationship unique or thematically significant. More layered than an explainer. Answers "what does this mean?" not just "how does this work?".

**Structure:**
1. Intro that frames the central observation — who is this character, what's the thing we're examining
2. Flowing paragraphs that build a layered argument — each paragraph adds a new layer
3. Bold inline for key conclusions
4. Blockquote for the single most important insight
5. Closing that elevates the observation — connects it to why the show works as a whole

**Tone:** Thoughtful and specific. References actual scenes, arcs, and moments. Avoid vague praise like "compelling" or "fascinating" — say exactly what makes it work and why.

**Example — What Makes Nezuko Impervious to Sunlight in Demon Slayer:**

At the heart of *Demon Slayer* is *Tanjiro Kamado*, the protagonist on a mission to eradicate demons. Central to the narrative is his younger sister, *Nezuko*, who was transformed into a demon by *Muzan Kibutsuji*. Demons typically wither away in daylight. Yet *Nezuko* defies this norm, earning her the moniker:

> 🤯 "The Chosen Demon."

While the exact reason for her immunity remains elusive, various theories attempt to unravel this mystery. Despite the *Kamado* family's lack of a direct connection to *Yoriichi Tsukiguni* — the original Demon Slayer and creator of the **Sun Breathing technique** — they incorporate Sun Breathing into an annual ritual.

This connection could explain why *Nezuko's* Blood Demon Art manifests as fire, **underscoring her ties to the Sun Breathing lineage.**

Remarkably, *Nezuko*, despite her demonic nature, **abstains from consuming human flesh**, distinguishing her as the only thriving demon without such a diet.

In a pivotal moment during the battle with *Upper Rank 4 Demon Hantengu*, *Nezuko* finds herself exposed to direct sunlight. While this scenario would typically prove fatal, **her resistance becomes evident.**

*Nezuko* stands out as **the only demon outside Muzan Kibutsuji's control**, joining *Tamayo* in this unique position. That independence — combined with her lineage's connection to Sun Breathing and her abstinence from human flesh — most likely explains her newfound immunity.

It's a layered piece of lore that rewards fans who pay attention, and it's one of the reasons *Demon Slayer* continues to pull people back in.
